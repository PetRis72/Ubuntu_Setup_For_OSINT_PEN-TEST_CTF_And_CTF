# 📸 Forensic Seizure Photography Checklist (2026 Standard)

In modern forensics, a photo is worth more than a thousand lines of code. These 10 photos provide "Visual Integrity," proving the state of the scene before, during, and after your intervention.

---

## 🛡️ The "10-Shot" Rule

Before touching any cables or power buttons, capture these 10 specific angles to prevent "Scene Tampering" accusations.

| Photo # | Subject | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | **The Wide Room Shot** | Establishes context. Shows everyone present and the overall environment. |
| **02** | **The Screen (Macro)** | Captures visible windows, open chats, or error messages. Do NOT move the mouse. |
| **03** | **System Clock vs. Atomic Clock** | Hold your forensic watch/phone next to the screen. Proves "Time Offset" for logs. |
| **04** | **Back of Computer (Cables)** | Proves what was plugged in (e.g., LAN, Mouse Jigglers, External Drives). |
| **05** | **Under/Side of Desk** | To find "hidden" hardware like hardware keyloggers or hidden Wi-Fi bridges. |
| **06** | **The Keyboard & Mouse** | Proves if "Rubber Duckies" or unauthorized USB devices were attached. |
| **07** | **Serial Number Labels** | Provides absolute identification of the hardware (Model, Make, Serial). |
| **08** | **The Power Source** | Shows if the device is on a UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) or a wall outlet. |
| **09** | **The Tamper Seal (Applied)** | Photo of the evidence bag/tape AFTER you have sealed the item. |
| **10** | **The "Exit" Shot** | Photo of the desk/area after the item is removed to show no damage was done. |

---

## 🛠️ 2026 Advanced Verification Steps

### **1. Metadata Integrity**
Ensure your forensic camera (or smartphone) has **GPS Tagging** enabled. In 2026, the metadata of your photos (EXIF data) acts as a secondary "Chain of Custody" for your location.

### **2. The "Atomic Clock" Sync**
Modern logs are useless if the computer's BIOS clock is drifting.
* **Photo #03** is critical. If the computer says `14:00:00` and the Atomic Clock says `14:00:12`, you must document a **+12 second offset**.

### **3. Live vs. Dead Documentation**
* **If Screen is ON:** Take a video of the screen for 10 seconds to show if any background processes or "Self-Destruct" scripts are running.
* **If Screen is OFF:** Photograph the "Dark Screen" to prove it was found in a powered-down state.

## ⚠️ Common Pitfalls

* **Using Flash on Screens:** Flash often makes the screen unreadable. Use ambient light or a side-angled flashlight.
* **Moving the Mouse:** In many 2026 security suites, moving the mouse triggers a "Lock on Disturbance" script. **Photograph the mouse, do not touch it.**
* **Reflections:** Be careful not to capture your own reflection in the monitor, as this can obscure details or be used to challenge your "neutral" presence.
